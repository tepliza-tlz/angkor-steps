The 3 Parts

1 · Claude AI

A Claude Project with a Knowledge Base (product list + stock rules). Produced two AI-written documents — a Stock SOP and a Monthly Stock Report — edited for Khmer context, plus a Prompt Log of 11+ prompts.

2 · Google Sheets

A spreadsheet "database" with 54 products, live formulas (stock value, status, current stock), a bar chart, and Apps Script that sends an automatic low-stock email alert.

3 · Website

A live, mobile-responsive admin dashboard with Dashboard analytics, Products, Stock In/Out, Sales history, and Settings — deployed on Netlify and saved in Git.

Key Features

Futuristic neon glassmorphism design with light and dark mode
Live dashboard: animated stat cards, category bar chart, inventory donut chart
Product search, filter by gender/category/low-stock, and sorting
Stock In / Stock Out that updates stock across the whole site and persists in the browser
Real sales history with units sold and revenue
Configurable low-stock threshold, riel exchange rate, and theme
Prices shown in US dollars with riel (≈ 4,100 ៛ = $1)

Tech Stack

Frontend: HTML, CSS (glassmorphism), vanilla JavaScript — no frameworks or external dependencies.
Data & storage: Google Sheets + Apps Script, browser localStorage.
AI: Claude (Project + Knowledge Base).
Hosting: Netlify, versioned on GitHub.
